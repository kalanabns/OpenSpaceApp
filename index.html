Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before OpenSpace launches.

What is OpenSpace?

OpenSpace is a mobile parking marketplace that connects drivers with suitable spaces offered by private owners.

Is the OpenSpace app available yet?

The app is preparing for launch. This page will be updated when a public download is available.

Can I reserve parking on this website?

No. This website provides information about OpenSpace. Reservations will take place in the mobile app.

What booking durations will the app support?

OpenSpace is designed to support hourly, daily, weekly and monthly parking. Available options depend on each space.

Can I offer my private parking space?

Suitable owners will be able to list space through the mobile app, subject to the relevant listing requirements.

How does the host commission work?

On a completed booking, the host receives 90% of the parking price and OpenSpace retains a 10% commission from host earnings.

Can I view amenities before booking?

Yes. The app is designed to show the details and amenities provided for each listed space before confirmation.

Where will OpenSpace launch initially?

OpenSpace's initial focus is Colombo, Sri Lanka. Specific coverage and the public launch date will be announced later.

How can I contact the team?

You can email support@openspace.lk.
