CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our upcoming release in Sri Lanka.

What is OpenSpace?

OpenSpace is a mobile parking marketplace that connects drivers with suitable parking spaces offered by private home and commercial property owners in Sri Lanka.

Is the OpenSpace app available yet?

The app is currently undergoing closed beta testing in Colombo. You can sign up on our early access waitlist above to be invited immediately when the public rollout opens.

Can I reserve parking on this website?

No. This website serves as the informational and early registration hub. All real-time slot searches, navigation passes, and secure payment processing operate natively inside the mobile app.

What booking durations will the app support?

OpenSpace supports flexible hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly parking arrangements, configured individually by each space owner to suit their schedule.

Can I offer my private parking space?

Yes! Eligible property owners in Colombo with residential driveways, secure front yards, or commercial customer slots can list space. Verification requires valid identification and proof of property rights.

How does the host commission work?

OpenSpace operates on a 90/10 revenue split. Space owners retain 90% of every completed booking tariff, while OpenSpace retains 10% to cover driver identity verification, mapping, customer support, and payment systems.

Can I view amenities before booking?

Yes. Every listing in the app details whether a space is covered, gated, monitored with CCTV, equipped with EV charging, or manned by on-site security before you confirm.

Where will OpenSpace launch initially?

OpenSpace is rolling out initially across Colombo (including Fort, Kollupitiya, Bambalapitiya, Slave Island, Cinnamon Gardens, and surrounding corridors) before expanding to other metropolitan hubs.

How can I contact the OpenSpace team?

You can email our team directly at support@openspace.lk for partnership, corporate bay, or host onboarding inquiries.
