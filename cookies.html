Cookie Information

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This page explains the cookies and outside services used on the OpenSpace landing page. The site does not currently include our own analytics or advertising trackers.

Google reCAPTCHA

We use Google reCAPTCHA on the beta invitation form to reduce spam and abuse. Google says reCAPTCHA sets a necessary _GRECAPTCHA cookie when it runs. Because the current widget loads from google.com, Google may also set other cookies. Read Google's reCAPTCHA FAQ and Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Google Fonts and Formspree

We load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to display them. When you submit the waitlist form, your browser sends the details you entered to Formspree. Our site does not use Formspree as an advertising tracker. Read our Privacy Policy for the details we collect and how long we keep them.

Your browser choices

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking reCAPTCHA or its necessary cookie may prevent the beta invitation form from working. You can email support@openspaceapp.lk if the form does not work for you.
