Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy covers the OpenSpace landing page and its beta invitation form. OpenSpace is currently an unregistered project based in Sri Lanka. For questions about your information or to ask for its removal, email support@openspaceapp.lk.

What we collect

When you join the beta waitlist, we collect the name, email address, location, and driver or parking space owner role that you enter. The form also uses Google reCAPTCHA to help prevent spam. Google may receive technical information about your browser and interaction with the page when reCAPTCHA loads.

Why we use it

We use your signup details only to review beta availability and send you an OpenSpace beta invitation. We rely on your consent, shown when you submit the form after reading its privacy notice. We do not use the waitlist for newsletters, unrelated promotions, or sales of your information.

How long we keep it

We remove your details from our active waitlist after sending an invitation, or no later than 30 days after you submit the form, whichever happens first. If we cannot invite you within 30 days, you may sign up again. If you ask us to remove your details sooner, we will remove the copies we control. Formspree and any other service providers may retain limited backup or security records under their own policies.

Services we use

Form submissions go to Formspree, which hosts its services with Amazon Web Services in the United States. We use Google reCAPTCHA to protect the form and Google Fonts to display the site. Those services may process technical information outside Sri Lanka. See our Cookie Information for more about reCAPTCHA.

Your choices

You can ask what signup details we hold, correct them, withdraw your request for an invitation, or ask us to remove them by emailing support@openspaceapp.lk. Please email from the address you used to sign up so we can find and verify your entry. You can also contact us with any privacy concern.

Changes to this policy

If our waitlist purpose, services, or retention period change, we will update this page before collecting new submissions under the changed terms. We will ask separately before using existing beta signups for unrelated messages.
